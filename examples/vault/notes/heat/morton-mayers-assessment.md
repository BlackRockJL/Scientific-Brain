Gist: Clear Fourier stability analysis for FTCS and Crank-Nicolson; thin on non-smooth initial data and on start-up strategies.

The von Neumann analysis matches the measured limit in [[ftcs-stability-limit]]. The treatment of oscillations for
large steps is brief; for that see [[cn-oscillations-large-time-step]], which rests on own runs.
